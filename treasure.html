Questions

Is the code the same for everyone?
Yes. The fragments are visible on each host page; you just have to read carefully.
Is there a deadline?
The hunt closes the moment a winner is drawn, or at the end of the month, whichever comes first. We will note the closing date here once it's set.
Can I enter more than once?
Once per person. Repeated submissions from the same email are treated as the first.
Will I be added to the newsletter mailing list?
No. The email you submit here is used only to schedule your call if you win. Subscribing to Still Hands is a separate, opt-in action.
