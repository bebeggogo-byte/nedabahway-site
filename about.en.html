ABOUT · IN ENGLISH

Kim Changhwan

Educator. Founder of Nedabahway. Jeju, South Korea.

Nedabahway is a one-person education studio based on Jeju Island, South Korea. It is run by Kim Changhwan — an educator, lecturer, and 1-on-1 coach — together with a homegrown swarm of ~60 software agents organized into 17 departments.

What we do

The studio works in four braided domains — career education, AI literacy, leadership, and facilitation — applied to the actual work context of one specific person at a time. The tagline: 한 사람의 일을 다시 디자인합니다 — We redesign one person's work.

Sixty minutes after a lecture ends, success means the learner walks out carrying one sentence: "Tomorrow at work, I will do one thing differently." Not information transfer; observation.

Core thesis

The essence of vocation is otherness. Work, at its base, is an act directed toward another person. The meaning of work is restored when it touches the unseen need of one specific person.
