## 1. Summary

The free-content layer delivers value but is a **funnel orphan**: the content
does not route visitors toward conversion. Of 579 free-content pages, **537
(93%) have no next-step CTA in the content body** — only the global nav/footer
boilerplate links out. The free-value hub, FAQ, and glossary do not exist.

The defect is concentrated, like S1: 506 of the 537 orphans are `resources/`
pages generated by `render_all.py` — one generator change wires them all.

## 2. Findings

### REQ-FV-005/006/007 — funnel-orphan free assets — Critical — FAIL

| Category | Has body CTA | Orphan (no CTA) |
|----------|--------------|-----------------|
| resources/ | 15 | 506 |
| auto/ (tools) | 0 | 15 |
| book/ | 1 | 5 |
| blog/ | 1 | 2 |
| scattered root pages | several | ~9 |
| **Total** | **42** | **537** |

A next-step CTA = a content-body link toward the free 30-minute consultation
or a relevant `/p/` program, distinct from the global nav/footer.

### REQ-FV-002/012/013/014 — missing hub, FAQ, glossary — Critical — FAIL

`free-value hub`, `faq.html`, and `glossary.html` do not exist. A cold visitor
has no single entry point to the free material, no FAQ, and no glossary for the
domain terms (STARCP, IDEN, 5S, WFO, 창직).

### REQ-FV-001 — corpus frozen — PASS

The free-content corpus is frozen at 579 pages
(`free-content-corpus.txt`) — the S1 public page set minus the conversion and
utility pages.
