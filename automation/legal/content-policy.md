# Operator Content Policy

Hard rules baked into the script-generation prompt and into operator review. Violations halt publish.

## Hard prohibitions (CRITICAL)

1. **No copyrighted audio.** Never include any trot recording, snippet, melody, or instrumental performance not generated under the operator's Suno Pro account.
2. **No real person photographs.** AI-generated illustrations only. Do not synthesize images that depict identifiable real artists in a way that could be mistaken for a photograph.
3. **No verbatim news copying.** Maximum 20 contiguous characters reused from any source. Always rephrase + add operator commentary.
4. **No defamation.** No claims about an artist's personal life, relationships, finances, or health unless those are publicly confirmed by the artist or the artist's official representatives.
5. **No fake quotes.** Never put words in an artist's mouth that they did not say in cited public materials.
6. **No deceptive thumbnails.** Thumbnail imagery must reflect actual video content.
7. **No medical / financial advice.** Senior audiences are vulnerable; the channel sticks to entertainment-information.
8. **No undisclosed sponsorship.** If any paid promotion enters Phase 4+, mark it per YouTube and 공정거래위원회 disclosure rules.

## Soft preferences (style)

- Neutral, warm tone. Senior-friendly vocabulary. No internet slang.
- Sentence length: average 10-18 Korean characters per clause; max 30.
- Avoid sensationalist superlatives ("최고", "유일한", "역대급") unless they are direct factual statements from cited sources.
- Names of artists: use stage name as the first reference, real name only if the artist uses it publicly.

## Pre-publish checklist (operator manual, Phase 1) / automated rubric (Phase 2+)

- [ ] Music is from `bgm_library` (operator-generated only).
- [ ] No real-person photos in any frame.
- [ ] Source URL listed in description.
- [ ] ≤20 contiguous characters from source.
- [ ] AI disclosure watermark visible.
- [ ] "Altered content" YouTube flag set.
- [ ] Takedown email present in description and channel About.
- [ ] Title is factual (no clickbait verbs).
- [ ] Audio loudness -16 LUFS ±1.

## Edge case decisions

| Situation | Decision |
|-----------|----------|
| Source article contains a direct artist quote | Quote may be included verbatim with quotation marks + source link, up to 50 chars. |
| Two sources disagree on a fact | Report both with attribution, do not pick a winner. |
| News about an artist's death | 24-hour publish hold; operator manual review required; tone shifted to memorial. |
| Anonymous tip-style content | Reject. Channel publishes only on cite-able sources. |
| Crime allegation against artist | Reject in Phase 1-3. Revisit policy at Phase 4 with legal counsel. |
